How it works

  1. Your photo is cropped to a square and scaled to the same grid as Jollibee.
  2. Every pixel is converted to OKLab. Similar colors get binned together.
  3. Source pixels are assigned to target spots — a permutation, never a recolor.
  4. Then they fly into place. Hold the result to peek at the original mascot.
