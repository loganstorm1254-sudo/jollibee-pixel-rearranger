How it works

  1. Your photo is center-cropped to a square and scaled to the target grid.
  2. Both images are converted to OKLab. Each pixel gets a sort key: luminance first, then hue.
  3. Source pixels and target positions are sorted by that key and zipped together — a permutation, not a recolor.
  4. Darkest pixels become outlines, brightest pixels become the hat and muzzle, everything else fills the red.
